Figure 4. Country-associated structure and inferred bacterial composition types in the cheese microbiome dataset. (A) Relative abundances of Lactococcus, Streptococcus, Lacticaseibacillus, Lactobacillus and Leuconostoc across 68 BioProjects with a single BioSample-reported sample-origin country and nonzero representation of the five genera. (B) Five descriptive bacterial composition types derived by k-means from within-BioProject normalized proportions of these genera. These groups are microbiome-derived composition types and do not represent experimentally verified starter-culture categories. (C) PERMANOVA effect sizes. The primary geographic analysis included 73 BioProjects from 20 countries (Country R2 = 0.366, p = 0.001); multivariate dispersion differed among countries (PERMDISP p = 0.001), and this result is therefore interpreted as country-associated community structure rather than an independent geographic effect. Restriction to countries represented by at least two independent BioProjects retained 63 BioProjects from 10 countries (Country R2 = 0.284, p = 0.001), while PERMDISP was not significant (p = 0.197). Composition-type models used the 68 BioProjects with nonzero representation of the five composition-associated genera. (D) Sensitivity analyses. The country association persisted after complete removal of the five composition-associated genera (R2 = 0.359, p = 0.001) and after adjustment for their continuous within-five-genus proportions (R2 = 0.306, p = 0.001). In 1,000 balanced subsamples containing three BioProjects from each of eight countries, PERMANOVA was significant in 99.4% of iterations, whereas PERMDISP was significant in 0% of iterations; the balanced median R2 is shown as an empirical sensitivity estimate rather than as the primary geographic effect size. Community analyses used Hellinger-transformed genus-level profiles, Bray-Curtis dissimilarity and 999 permutations.
